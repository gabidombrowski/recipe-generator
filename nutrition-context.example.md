# Nutrition context

Copy this to `nutrition-context.md` (gitignored) and replace it with your own
notes, or just edit it in the app at `/context`.

This file is free text on purpose. It holds the things that don't belong in a
schema — the reasons behind the numbers, what's actually been working, what to
raise at the next check-in. The app renders it and writes back to it; nothing
here is parsed.

## Goal

What you're actually training for, and by when.

## What's working

Meals that have felt good, timings that fit the week, anything worth repeating.

## What isn't

Dishes that sat badly, days the plan didn't survive contact with reality.

## Constraints worth remembering

Intolerances, medication timing, travel weeks, anything that explains an
exclusion on the Kitchen page.

## Questions for the next check-in

Things to ask a coach, dietitian or doctor.
